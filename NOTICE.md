# Third-party content

The MIT licence in `LICENSE` covers the source code only.

The photographs in `app/img/` come from Wikimedia Commons and Wikipedia and
remain under their own licences — mostly CC BY-SA and public domain. Author,
licence and source URL for each of the 272 photos are recorded in
`app/img/credits.json`, and the app shows them under
Settings → For grown-ups → Photo credits. Reusing or redistributing the photos
means honouring those terms, which generally requires keeping the attribution.

The Fredoka font in `app/fonts/` is licensed under the SIL Open Font License.
