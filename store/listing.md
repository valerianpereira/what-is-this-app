## Short description (80 chars max)

    Picture words for 2–4 year olds. Look, say it out loud, hear it back.

## Full description

    Show a picture, let your child say the word, and five seconds later the
    app says it back. That is the whole game — and it is exactly what 2–4
    year olds love doing.

    HOW IT WORKS
    • Pick a group: Animals, Fruits, Vehicles, Body parts, Toys, Colours,
      Shapes and more — 22 groups, 545 words.
    • A big photo appears. Your child names it out loud. No tapping needed.
    • A gentle countdown, then the app says the word clearly.
    • Ten pictures a round, then a little cheer and a list of the words to
      tap and hear again.

    MADE FOR SMALL HANDS
    • Huge pictures, one thing on screen at a time.
    • Real photographs, not cartoons — the child learns what a real pear or
      a real tractor looks like.
    • Nothing to buy, nothing to unlock, no pop-ups, no ads.
    • Settings and the Parent zone sit behind a two-second press-and-hold.

    FOR GROWN-UPS
    • Choose how long the child gets to think: 3, 5 or 8 seconds.
    • Turn the voice off to say the words yourself.
    • See which words have been seen and how many rounds were played.
    • Download every picture once and play offline — on the plane, in the
      car, anywhere.

    PRIVACY
    • No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no data leaves the phone.
    • Uses the internet only to fetch the card photos the first time.
    • Open source: github.com/valerianpereira/what-is-this-app

## Release

    npm run aab            # builds and signs dist/what-is-this.aab

Upload `dist/what-is-this.aab` under Production → Create new release. Play
App Signing is on by default for new apps: Play keeps the app signing key and
`android/upload-keystore.jks` is only the upload key. Bump `versionCode` in
`android/app/build.gradle` before every upload.

## Testing requirement

New personal developer accounts must run a closed test with 12 testers for 14
days before production access is granted. Create a Closed testing track first,
upload the same AAB there, and share the opt-in link.
